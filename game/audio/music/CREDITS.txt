Music in Mind the School
========================

All tracks by Purrple Cat, from the Free Music Archive:
https://freemusicarchive.org/music/purrple-cat/

The files were converted from MP3 to Ogg Vorbis. Nothing else was changed:
no editing, no volume change, no cuts. The game plays some tracks at a lower
volume or stops them before trailing silence, without changing the files.
