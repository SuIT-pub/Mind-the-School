Ambience in Mind the School
===========================

All ambience loops come from Freesound (https://freesound.org/). For the
game each recording was cut to a loop (the end is crossfaded into the start),
resampled to 48 kHz, levelled to -27 LUFS and converted to Ogg Vorbis. The
gym loop also has its loudest ball bounces softened by a limiter.
The unedited downloads are kept outside the game folder.
