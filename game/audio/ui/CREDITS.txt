Interface sounds in Mind the School
===================================

All interface sounds come from Freesound (https://freesound.org/). For the
game each recording was cut, faded, resampled to 48 kHz, changed in volume
and converted to Ogg Vorbis. The unedited downloads are kept outside the
game folder.

journal-open.ogg, journal-close.ogg
  "Open and Close a Diary Book" by Sabacky
  https://freesound.org/people/Sabacky/sounds/774136/
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 - https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changed: split into the opening and the closing half, volume changed.

page-turn.ogg
  "Page Turn 01" by LilMati
  https://freesound.org/people/LilMati/sounds/397548/
  Licence: CC0 1.0

choice.ogg, notify.ogg, journal-entry.ogg
  "Pencil scribble on paper" by MJM94
  https://freesound.org/people/MJM94/sounds/859147/
  Licence: CC0 1.0
  choice and notify are single strokes cut from it, journal-entry is the whole take.

collect.ogg
  "Deal.ogg" by egomassive, made from "Card Flip" by F4ngy
  https://freesound.org/people/egomassive/sounds/536784/
  Licence: CC BY 3.0 - https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  Changed: resampled, volume changed.

gift.ogg
  "Leo Ming l Stone In Focus l Chimes5 - Bm" by Stone_in_Focus_FILMish
  https://freesound.org/people/Stone_in_Focus_FILMish/sounds/866461/
  Licence: CC0 1.0
  Starts at the bright chime 1.9 s into the recording.

milestone.ogg
  "26.07.24- Tibetan Singing Bowl - Octave Pitch-Shifted Scale" by Valerie-Vivegnis
  https://freesound.org/people/Valerie-Vivegnis/sounds/864397/
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 - https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changed: only the first of the seven strikes (G5), faded out, volume changed.
