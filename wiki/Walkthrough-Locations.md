Regular (sandbox) events by building. Story-chain scenes that *also* live on
these actions are linked rather than duplicated.

**Stat notes** are typical directions, not exact numbers — modifiers and school
level change the size of the swing. `+` / `-` mean small, more marks mean
larger.

Time shorthand: **FT** = free time (morning, noon, evening), **CT** = class
time (early noon, early afternoon, afternoon), **Day** = all daytime slots.

<a id="other-buildings"></a>
## Other buildings

**Labs**, **sports field**, **bath**, **beach**, **swimming pool**, and **staff
lodges** exist on the map. They currently have almost no regular event pools —
mostly backgrounds, tutorials, and [lab intro](Walkthrough-Event-Chains#lab-intro)
search beats.

## Situations that inject extra scenes

These are **not** location events you hunt by name. While the situation is
active they can replace or join the pool:

| Situation | Where extra scenes show up |
|-----------|----------------------------|
| [New Management](Walkthrough-Event-Chains#new-management) | Office look around / call secretary / work; courtyard patrol / search; school patrol / check class / teach; kiosk snack |
| Cafeteria Crisis | Cafeteria and related (WIP thresholds) |
| Body Conflict | Gym changing room, cafeteria, dorm peek, courtyard, teaching, counselling (WIP thresholds) |
