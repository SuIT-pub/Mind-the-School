## How to use these pages

Story events only fire when **time, location, and prerequisites** all match.
If a step does not appear, check the weekday, the time of day, and whether the
previous scene actually finished (not just started).

Most campus events are **random pulls** from a pool. Repeat the same action at
the same time of day until the one you need comes up. Highlighted map actions
are story beats that are ready now.

## Time of day

| Slot | Name | Typical use |
|------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Morning | Free time |
| 2 | Early noon | Class time (PE, lessons) |
| 3 | Noon | Free time |
| 4 | Early afternoon | Class time |
| 5 | Afternoon | Class time |
| 6 | Evening | Free time |
| 7 | Night | Dorms, courtyard patrol |

**Class time** is early noon + early afternoon + afternoon (weekdays).
**Free time** is morning + noon + evening.
**Daytime** means anything except night.

Weekdays are Monday–Friday. Weekends are Saturday–Sunday.

## Suggested order

1. **First week** — visit every building, then the first PTA on day 5.
2. **First potion** — day 9 walkaround, then the hangover settles.
3. **[New Management](Walkthrough-Event-Chains#new-management)** — keep the
   school from sliding while you settle in as headmaster.
4. Teach **Class 3A** once (**Teach Class** in the school building, class time).
5. **[Aona's sports bra](Walkthrough-Event-Chains#aonas-sports-bra)** — needs
   that first class, then **Teach P.E.**
6. **[First time naughty](Walkthrough-Event-Chains#first-time-naughty)** — three
   counselling sessions, then Emiko.
7. **[Sex ed introduction](Walkthrough-Event-Chains#sex-ed-introduction)** —
   Emiko at level 6 and Aona's shopping trip done.
8. In parallel, whenever they come up:
   **[cafeteria](Walkthrough-Unlocks#cafeteria)**,
   **[school jobs](Walkthrough-Unlocks#school-jobs)**,
   **[student relationships](Walkthrough-Unlocks#student-relationships)**.
9. From school **level 2**:
   **[yoga outfits](Walkthrough-Event-Chains#new-yoga-outfit)**,
   **[truth or dare](Walkthrough-Event-Chains#truth-or-dare)**,
   **[lab intro](Walkthrough-Event-Chains#lab-intro)**.

Higher school levels (3–10) currently use **temporary journal unlocks**. Not
every event has art or variants at those levels yet. What each level *means*
(dress, talk, behaviour) is on **[School Levels](School-Levels)**.
