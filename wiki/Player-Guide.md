## School levels

The campus has a **school level** from **1** (modest, sex is unspeakable) to
**10** (sexual anarchy). It changes how students dress, how they talk, and what
happens in public versus behind a door.

Full names, dress, talk, and behaviour for each step:
**[School Levels](School-Levels)**.

How the number moves in play (sex-ed jump to 2; temporary PTA votes for 3–10):
**[Walkthrough — Unlocks](Walkthrough-Unlocks#school-levels)**.

Outfit cards on each [character page](Characters) are labeled Level 00–10 on
that same scale.
