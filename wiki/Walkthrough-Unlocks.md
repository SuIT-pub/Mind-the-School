How journal **rules** and **buildings** become available. After the finding
event you still have to **schedule a PTA vote** and win the factions.

The journal unlockable system was rebuilt recently. Right now the only fully
wired unlockable *objects* are the temporary **school level** votes. Cafeteria,
school jobs, student relationships, and theoretical sex ed still **unlock via
story flags** ("Added new rule/building to journal!") and then go to a vote.
The finding steps below are current; the vote UI may still be in flux.

<a id="cafeteria"></a>
## Cafeteria (building)

The school has no working canteen. Students live off the kiosk.

**Finding event:** Kiosk → **Get a Snack**, **Expensive Bento**.

| | |
|--|--|
| When | Weekdays free time, or weekends daytime |
| Extra | 65% chance to even be in the pool; school **level 4 or below**; cafeteria not already open |

[Miwa Igarashi](Miwa-Igarashi) cannot afford a bento. Two choices:

- **Help out** — you pay **$50**. Happiness and reputation go up. This does
  **not** unlock the cafeteria.
- **Leave alone** — the clerk's reaction is rolled:
  - **Normal** — he refuses; small happiness/charm hit
  - **Kind** (25%) — he gives her the bento. You realise students are going
    hungry. **Cafeteria is added to the journal.**
  - **Slimy** (5%) — he offers something else entirely; large happiness/charm
    hit and a reputation ding

The old wiki said "leave her alone and hope for 25%". That is still the route.
Helping her is the kind choice for *her*, not the unlock.

After the vote passes, the building spends time **under construction**. Visiting
it during that window plays a short "still building" beat, then it opens.

The **Cafeteria Crisis** situation in the journal is a longer Adelaide/kitchen
arc on top of this. Several of its thresholds are still placeholders — use it
for flavour and injected events, not as a required walkthrough.

<a id="school-jobs"></a>
## School jobs (rule)

Students can work in school facilities (starting with the cafeteria).

**Finding event:** Cafeteria → **Order Food**, **A lot to do**, while the rule
is not yet unlocked. Weekdays, daytime.

[Adelaide Hall](Adelaide-Hall) is running the counter alone. Variants:

| Variant | What happens |
|---------|----------------|
| Normal | She copes; small parent-facing happiness/charm |
| Tripped | She falls; mixed charm/reputation |
| Overwhelmed | She is drowning in tickets |

You need the **overwhelmed** variant **three times**. On the fourth overwhelmed
visit you step behind the counter, spend two hours on dishes, and propose hiring
students. Adelaide backs it at the PTA. **School Jobs** is added to the journal.

After the vote, **Students at work** replaces this event at the counter
(weekdays morning/evening, weekends daytime, school level 1–6). Miwa, plus
sometimes Elsie or Luna, plus sometimes Sakura.

<a id="student-relationships"></a>
## Student relationships (rule)

Lets students date and show it on campus. Until then, couples get sent to the
office.

**Finding event:** Office → **Look around**, **Two students waiting**.
Weekdays, daytime. School level 1–5, rule not yet unlocked.

[Yuriko Oshima](Yuriko-Oshima) and [Elsie Johnson](Elsie-Johnson) are sitting
there because a teacher caught them as a couple.

1. Do **not** ignore them — that only ticks teacher happiness.
2. **Ask why they are here.**
3. **Take care of it for them** — you tell them the rule is on the way out.
   **Student relationships** is added to the journal.

**Tell about policy** lectures them and does *not* unlock the rule.

Once the vote passes, the **Couple kissing** patrol event in the school
building no longer offers "Stop them" as the policy option.

<a id="theoretical-sex-ed"></a>
## Theoretical sex education (rule)

New subject: bodies and reproduction, on paper. Finding this is the whole
**[Sex ed introduction](Walkthrough-Event-Chains#sex-ed-introduction)** chain.
The journal entry appears at the PTA discussion, before the vote.

After it passes you get the Friday assembly, the weekend mini-events, and the
Monday first class. **Teach Class → Sex Ed** stays in the school building
afterwards.

<a id="school-levels"></a>
## School levels 3–10 (temporary)

There is no proper level-up story yet. The journal lists a single **Level**
unlockable that steps from 3 to 10.

- Visible from school **level 2** (for the jump to 3).
- Each vote **sets** school, parent, and teacher levels, and bumps Emiko.
- The description warns you to **back up the save**. Not every event has
  variants at high levels.

This is a stopgap. It will be replaced by real transition events.

What each number *means* on campus (dress, talk, behaviour from modest to
anarchy) is the **[School Levels](School-Levels)** bible. Story currently
reaches **2** via [sex-ed introduction](Walkthrough-Event-Chains#sex-ed-introduction);
the votes above skip the missing transition scenes.

## PTA votes in general

Unlocking an option in the journal is not the same as passing it.

1. Open the journal, schedule the vote, and pick a meeting.
2. Raise **teachers / parents / students** support (work the office,
   patrol, teach, counselling — whatever the factions care about).
3. Show up to the PTA. Dedicated vote scenes exist for theoretical sex ed;
   other votes use the generic PTA flow.

Money costs, when they exist, are charged at schedule time and refunded if you
cancel.
