## Appearance

Long auburn-red hair worn in a voluminous, glamorous side-swept style — shorter
layered pieces up top swept to one side, the wavy length cascading over one
shoulder. Blue-grey eyes, fair skin, and a slim, elegant figure with a moderate
bust. Soft pink lips, no standing jewellery.

**Uniform across the levels.** Kokoro's take on the uniform is modest at first but
turns notably mature and edgy — she reaches for grown-up outerwear rather than
cutesy schoolgirl pieces. At level 1 it's a plain white blouse and plaid pleated
skirt, neat and covered; by level 5 a navy ribbed cardigan worn open in a deep V
with little underneath; at level 6 a black leather biker jacket over a grey lace
bralette; by level 10 a cropped bolero left open over bare skin above a belted
plaid micro-skirt. Paired with the glamorous hair, it's a more sophisticated,
grown-up look than most of her classmates wear — a slightly surprising contrast
with her timid streak.

## Personality

Shy, modest, and quick to fluster — when she's pushed out of her comfort zone
(an embarrassing outfit, an awkward moment) she wells up rather than pushes back.
Yet there's more to her than the timidity: a quiet, grown-up sense of style and a
poise that make her read as more composed and elegant than she actually feels
inside. And underneath it all she's genuinely curious, asking frank, almost
clinical questions about sex and the body once the subject is opened.

**Level progression.** At low levels the shyness dominates and mortification comes
easily, even as her look stays a step more sophisticated than her classmates'. As
the campus climate rises (see [School Levels](School-Levels)) her curiosity — and
that understated elegance — increasingly win out over the embarrassment, though she
never becomes brazen about it.
