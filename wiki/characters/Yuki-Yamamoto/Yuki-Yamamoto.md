**Parent**

Yuki Yamamoto is a parent character and the mother of
[Soyoon Yamamoto](Soyoon-Yamamoto).

## Description

- Height: 170.9 cm
- Bra size: 65DD (E)
- Bust–waist–hips: 77–67–94 cm
- Waist-to-hips: 0.713
- Mother of [Soyoon Yamamoto](Soyoon-Yamamoto)
