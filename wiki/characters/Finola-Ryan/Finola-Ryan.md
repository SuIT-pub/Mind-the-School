**Teacher**

Finola Ryan teaches English and Geography.

## Description

- Height: 169.0 cm
- Bra size: 65DD (E)
- Bust–waist–hips: 78–65–88 cm
- Waist-to-hips: 0.745
- Subjects: English, Geography

## Appearance

Short auburn-red hair in a tousled chin-length bob with side-swept bangs, fair
skin with a light scatter of freckles, and blue eyes. She wears a pair of pale-blue
teardrop earrings at all times, even out of her clothes. Her figure is athletic and
toned — a fit, defined midriff over a moderate-full bust and curvy hips — younger
and sportier-looking than her role might suggest (she's only 28). Note she shares
the red hair and freckles with student [Easkey Tanaka](Easkey-Tanaka), but wears
hers in a much shorter bob and is far more toned.

**Signature look across the levels.** As a teacher Finola wears her own clothes,
and her taste runs bright, youthful, and casual rather than formal — a consistent
yellow-and-blue palette with black lace-up boots she keeps at every level. At
level 1 it's a yellow off-shoulder top and high-waisted blue jeans; by level 5 a
yellow tank over low-rise jeans with lingerie peeking at the hip; by level 10 the
top is tugged down off her chest above tiny denim booty-shorts with a heart-buckle
belt. The blue earrings and the boots are the constants. It reads as an
approachable, relatable-young-teacher look rather than a stuffy one — which fits
how she visibly loosens up over time.

## Personality

Dutiful, methodical, and conscientious. Finola takes her responsibilities as a
teacher seriously and does things properly; she's a touch reserved and easily
embarrassed, but fundamentally honest — she won't lie even when the truthful
answer costs her composure.

**Level progression.** At low levels she's prim and proper, blushes at the
subject, but answers straight when pressed. As the campus climate rises (see
[School Levels](School-Levels)), the primness erodes while the diligence stays:
she'll participate and demonstrate because it's her job and she's thorough about
her job, the reserve giving way to a matter-of-fact, almost clinical
thoroughness. Her professionalism is the constant thread across every level.
