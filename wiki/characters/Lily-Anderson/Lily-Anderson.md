**Teacher**

Lily Anderson teaches Math and Sciences.

## Description

- Height: 167.0 cm
- Bra size: 70B
- Bust–waist–hips: 76–64–90 cm
- Waist-to-hips: 0.705
- Subjects: Math, Sciences

## Appearance

Long, soft auburn hair, wavy and centre-parted, falling past her shoulders; blue
eyes, fair skin (no freckles), and a slim figure with a moderate bust. Of the
several redheads on staff and in class she's the one with the longest, softest
waves — distinct from [Finola Ryan](Finola-Ryan)'s short freckled bob. Pinkish
lips, minimal jewellery.

**Signature look across the levels.** As a teacher Lily wears her own clothes in a
consistent purple palette — lilac tops, purple hosiery, and purple flats at every
level. At level 1 she's fully professional: a charcoal pinstripe blazer with purple
trim over a lilac blouse, a black pencil skirt, and purple tights — the put-together
look of a conscientious teacher. By level 5 it's an unbuttoned lilac blouse over a
blue mini with purple thigh-highs; by level 10 a cropped lilac bolero baring her
chest over tiny blue shorts, the purple stockings and flats still on. The colour
and the neatness are the constants — she stays coordinated even as the coverage
goes.

## Personality

Conscientious, anxious, and overworked. Lily grades past midnight, runs herself
toward burnout, and worries — her instinct on sex education is a conservative
"won't this just make them worse?" She cares deeply about her students and
carries it heavily, keeping a composed professional register that's distinct from
how frayed she is underneath. When she's stretched too thin the composure cracks
into snappishness and a quiet bitterness — a sense that nobody sees how much she
carries — and she can turn short-tempered and resentful before she catches herself.
She teaches Math and Sciences.

**Level progression.** Her worry and dutifulness are constant. As the campus
climate rises (see [School Levels](School-Levels)) she's pulled along anxiously
rather than eagerly, still fretting over consequences the others have stopped
noticing.
