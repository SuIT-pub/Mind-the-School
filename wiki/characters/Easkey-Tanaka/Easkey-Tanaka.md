**Class 3A · Student**

Easkey Tanaka is a student in class 3A. She is in a relationship with
[Sakura Mori](Sakura-Mori).

## Description

- Height: 168.8 cm
- Bra size: 65DD (E)
- Bust–waist–hips: 78–68–98 cm
- Waist-to-hips: 0.689
- In a relationship with [Sakura Mori](Sakura-Mori)

## Appearance

Coppery auburn-red hair, wavy and tousled, worn in a loose low side-tail with
face-framing bangs. Her most distinctive feature is a heavy dusting of freckles
across her nose, cheeks, and shoulders. Blue-green eyes, fair skin, and a soft,
curvy figure — a full bust, a gently rounded tummy, and wide hips and thighs,
softer and less toned than most of her classmates. Minimal makeup, no jewellery.

**Uniform across the levels.** Easkey wears the sailor-style variant of the school
uniform — a white middy top with a sailor collar and red neckerchief over the
blue-and-red plaid pleated skirt. She keeps it plain and doesn't dress it up; what
changes across the levels is only how much of it survives. At level 1 it's neat and
fully covered; by level 5 the top has gone sheer over a white lace bra and the
skirt shrunk to a micro; by level 10 it's reduced to the collar and tie draped over
bare breasts, with plaid booty-shorts and a yellow belt below. Even stripped down
there's no added flourish — the plainness suits her unassuming, shy streak.

## Personality

Shy, gentle, and easily flustered — Easkey stammers when she's put on the spot
and would rather not be noticed. She's frank about disliking schoolwork ("why do
I need to learn this anyway?") and isn't especially driven academically. She's
in a steady relationship with [Sakura Mori](Sakura-Mori), who tends to be the
more capable, grounded half of the pair.

**Level progression.** At low levels her shyness reads as modesty — she wants
people to knock, embarrasses easily. As the campus climate rises (see
[School Levels](School-Levels)) she loosens up in Sakura's company, but stays one
of the softer, more retiring girls rather than a bold one.
