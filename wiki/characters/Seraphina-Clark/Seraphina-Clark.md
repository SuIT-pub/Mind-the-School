## Personality

The more forward of the Clark twins — active, socially confident, and a prankster
at heart. Seraphina is somewhat precocious and quick with a teasing joke, and she's
the loud, visible half of the chaos-and-pranks duo she forms with her twin
[Luna](Luna-Clark): Seraphina fronts the mischief and takes the lead, while quiet
Luna is often the one actually scheming behind her. She'll happily be the grinning
face of a prank Luna cooked up.

**Level progression.** Even at the lowest levels she reads as more open and
curious than her peers — but "open" is not "explicit": at level 1 she's still
fundamentally prudish and innocent, just less shy about it. She tends to reach
each new stage of the campus climate (see [School Levels](School-Levels))
slightly early, pulling the more hesitant Luna along after her.
