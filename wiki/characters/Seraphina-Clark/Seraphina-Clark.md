**Class 3A · Student**

Seraphina Clark is a student in class 3A. She is the twin sister of
[Luna Clark](Luna-Clark).

## Description

- Height: 144.8 cm
- Bra size: 55DD (E)
- Bust–waist–hips: 68–48–81 cm
- Waist-to-hips: 0.587
- Twin sister of [Luna Clark](Luna-Clark)
- More active and open than her sister
- A bit of a jokester
