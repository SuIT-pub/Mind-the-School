## Personality

Often grumpy and short-spoken, but with a heart of gold. Linh is quite shy and
not one for social niceties — what she lacks in people skills she more than makes
up for in sheer sense of duty. She gives her absolute all when treating her
patients and steps up without fuss when she's needed (she comes in to run the
students' health checkups, doing the first round for free). If there's one person
you can truly trust, it's her.

A nurse at the hospital in the city and a friend of [Zoe Parker](Zoe-Parker).
