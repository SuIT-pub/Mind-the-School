**Administrative staff · Nurse**

Linh Nguyen is the nurse. She works at the hospital in the city.

## Description

- Height: 165.6 cm
- Bra size: 60C
- Bust–waist–hips: 68–70–95 cm
- Waist-to-hips: 0.738
- Nurse at the hospital in the city

## Appearance

Tanned, olive skin — one of only two characters with a sun-kissed tone (the other
being parent [Nubia Davis](Nubia-Davis)) — with a short
blue-black bob, side-swept bangs, and warm amber-brown eyes. Freckles across her
nose and cheeks, and a pair of red tasselled earrings she keeps on throughout.
Slim and athletic, with a smaller bust and curvy hips.

**Signature look across the levels.** Linh dresses in medical whites with red
accents — her nurse's kit *is* her wardrobe, and the white-and-red theme (with
white trainers) never changes. At level 1 it's a proper white nurse's tunic over
white trousers, tidy and clinical; by level 6 a sheer white zip-front nurse dress
over a blue bikini; at level 8 the same dress worn sheer over nothing; by level 10
an outright naughty-nurse outfit — an underbust white-and-red crop and a pleated
micro-skirt with a heart-buckle belt. However far it goes, she stays recognisably
the nurse — whites, red cross-and-heart accents, sensible shoes.

## Personality

Often grumpy and short-spoken, but with a heart of gold. Linh is quite shy and
not one for social niceties — what she lacks in people skills she more than makes
up for in sheer sense of duty. She gives her absolute all when treating her
patients and steps up without fuss when she's needed (she comes in to run the
students' health checkups, doing the first round for free). If there's one person
you can truly trust, it's her.

A nurse at the hospital in the city and a friend of [Zoe Parker](Zoe-Parker).
