**Teacher**

Zoe Parker teaches Physical Education and Health.

## Description

- Height: 167.3 cm
- Bra size: 65C
- Bust–waist–hips: 73–63–91 cm
- Waist-to-hips: 0.692
- Subjects: Physical Education, Health

## Appearance

Short, choppy blonde hair with side-swept bangs, blue eyes, and freckles across her
nose and cheeks. Fair skin and a fit, athletic figure — toned, with a moderate-full
bust and curvy hips. Her most distinctive mark is a large black tattoo across her
stomach: a butterfly wreathed in baroque filigree.

**Signature look across the levels.** As the PE-and-Health teacher, Zoe lives in
athletic wear, and it stays sporty all the way up — the same red running trainers
throughout. At level 1 it's a full red velour tracksuit, zipped to the neck; by
level 5 the track jacket hangs open over a lime competition swimsuit and gym shorts;
at level 7 just the high-cut racing swimsuit (its front cutout framing the belly
tattoo) with shorts; by level 10 a tiny string bikini and a strappy thong. Whatever
the level it reads as gymwear rather than fashion — coach through and through, which
fits her hands-on, active streak.

## Personality

The conscience of the staff. Zoe is caring, protective, and safety-first with
her students — she'll push back on the Headmaster directly when she thinks he's
gone too far ("don't you think that is a bit too much?"). Professional and
proactive: she runs her PE and yoga classes with real duty of care ("don't push
too hard, it's not a competition"), and solves problems by reaching out — she
brings in her nurse friend [Linh Nguyen](Linh-Nguyen) for the students' checkups,
arranged for free.

**Level progression.** Her protective instinct is the constant. As the campus
climate rises (see [School Levels](School-Levels)) she's the staff member most
likely to keep worrying about the girls' wellbeing even as the culture around her
stops.
