**Class 3A · Student**

Aona Komuro is a student in class 3A.

## Description

- Height: 172.5 cm
- Bra size: 75F (DDD)
- Bust–waist–hips: 89–74–98 cm
- Waist-to-hips: 0.756

## Appearance

Long, wavy pale-blonde hair worn loose with a centre part, falling in soft waves
past her shoulders. Fair skin, pale blue eyes, and a soft, youthful face with
natural, barely-there makeup. Her figure is tall and strongly hourglass — a very
full bust over a narrow waist and wide hips and thighs — the same generous chest
that, by her own account, gives her back trouble. She wears no particular
jewellery.

**Uniform across the levels.** Like every student Aona wears the standard school
uniform — white blouse and red-and-blue plaid pleated skirt — so what marks her
out is her *variation* of it rather than any separate wardrobe: how much she keeps
on and how she wears it. At level 1 it's neat and fully buttoned over a modest
pleated mini; by level 5 the blouse is knotted up to bare her midriff and falls
open over a red bra, the skirt shortened to a micro; by level 10 the blouse is
sheer and the skirt barely a pelmet. Tellingly, she doesn't personalise or
accessorise it the way a more fashion-minded girl would — she wears the uniform
plainly, just progressively barer.

## Personality

Loud, brash, and hungry for attention. Aona needs to be the center of the
room and works for it — through boasting, teasing, or sheer volume. She
competes at everything, over-shares constantly, and can't give a short answer
when a long story will hold the floor longer. She's quick to anger and quick to
envy, too — let someone outshine her or steal the attention and she flares up on
the spot, the temper pure wounded pride. Under the bravado she's more
fragile than she lets on — and specifically insecure about her own body. Her
very large chest causes her real back pain and embarrassment; early on she'd
rather cover up than show off, and she's quietly grateful to anyone who treats
the problem with care. Her cruelty toward [Miwa Igarashi](Miwa-Igarashi) over
*small* breasts is that same insecurity turned outward — two girls unhappy with
their chests, lashing in opposite directions.

**Level progression.** At low levels her craving for attention is purely
social, and it has a mean edge — early in the game it surfaces as cruelty, most
notably teasing [Miwa Igarashi](Miwa-Igarashi) over her small chest (a conflict
that should be resolved by roughly level 3–4). As the campus climate rises (see
[School Levels](School-Levels)), that same need for eyes reroutes into
sexuality: she becomes the first to volunteer, the one who wants to be watched,
and by the higher levels a full exhibitionist. The engine — loud, competitive,
insecure — never changes; only where she points it does.
