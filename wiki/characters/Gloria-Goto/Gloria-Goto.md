## Personality

An analytical nerd, endlessly and bluntly curious. Gloria treats bodies, sex,
and other people's private lives as fascinating subject matter to be catalogued
and understood. Her default register is a flat, clinical deadpan — but let a
genuinely interesting data point surface and the deadpan cracks into eager,
over-explaining rambling. She asks the questions everyone else is too
embarrassed to voice, purely because she wants to know. Her curiosity has no real
brakes, which is the unsettling part: ethics come a distant second to knowing, and
she can treat the people she studies as data points rather than friends — cool and
detached in a way that unnerves them.

**Level progression.** At low levels her curiosity is academic and innocent —
she asks blunt biological questions that mortify the room without meaning
anything by them. As the campus climate rises (see [School Levels](School-Levels)),
the same detached curiosity turns toward sex directly: she surveys, records,
experiments, wants the numbers. Her own arousal, when it comes, never interrupts
the analysis — she'll narrate her own mechanics as calmly as anyone else's. The
constant is detachment-as-lens.
