**Class 3A · Student**

Soyoon Yamamoto is a student in class 3A. She is the daughter of
[Yuki Yamamoto](Yuki-Yamamoto).

## Description

- Height: 161.0 cm
- Bra size: 60C
- Bust–waist–hips: 68–91–92 cm
- Waist-to-hips: 0.664
- Daughter of [Yuki Yamamoto](Yuki-Yamamoto)
