## Personality

Creative, expressive, and quietly rebellious under a careful professional
surface. The tattoos and the rocker wardrobe are the real Chloe — an artist
through and through. In front of parents and colleagues, though, her first
instinct is decorum and "what will the community think"; that caution is a
professional reflex, the face an art-and-music teacher keeps up at a conservative
school, not her true self. She's reasonable and quick to be won over by a good
argument, because she's rarely as scandalised as she lets on.

**Level progression.** At low levels she plays the propriety card and acts as one
of the brakes on anything explicit — keeping up appearances. As the campus climate
rises (see [School Levels](School-Levels)) she needs that front less and less, and
her expressive, unbothered real self comes through faster than a genuinely
conservative colleague's would. The mask drops; the artist stays.
