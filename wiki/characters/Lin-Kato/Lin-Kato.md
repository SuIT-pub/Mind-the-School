**Class 3A · Student**

Lin Kato is a student in class 3A.

## Description

- Height: 167.6 cm
- Bra size: 65B
- Bust–waist–hips: 70–63–94 cm
- Waist-to-hips: 0.663

## Appearance

Black hair with a faint blue sheen, worn in a high side ponytail with a swept
fringe; bright green eyes and a scatter of freckles across her nose and cheeks.
Fair skin and a slim, athletic figure — a toned midriff, a smaller bust, and
curvy hips. Natural makeup, no standing jewellery.

**Uniform across the levels.** Lin wears the uniform in a relaxed, casual-sporty
way that matches her easygoing streak — nothing fussed over. At level 1 it's smart
enough: a navy gold-button blazer over a white blouse and the plaid pleated skirt;
by level 5 the blouse is knotted up at the midriff over a bra with the sleeves
rolled, casual and undone; by level 10 a cropped open jacket over a tiny
chain-trimmed plaid micro-skirt. She reads as the laid-back, throw-it-on type
rather than someone curating a look.

## Personality

Sociable, easygoing, and funny — Lin is the one who keeps the fun going. She
organises the games, eggs everyone on, cracks jokes, and loves dumb internet cat
videos. Practical and unpretentious, she's a bit of a ringleader in her friend
group, though she'll deflect with a quick "I'm not that kind of person" when a
dare cuts too close.

**Level progression.** As the campus climate rises (see [School Levels](School-Levels))
her ringleader energy carries straight over — she's game, loud about it, and the
one daring everyone else on, without much hang-up.
