**Parent**

Nubia Davis is a parent character.

## Description

- Height: 169.3 cm
- Bra size: 75DD (E)
- Bust–waist–hips: 88–68–97 cm
- Waist-to-hips: 0.697

## Appearance

Short, choppy white-silver hair with side-swept bangs, tanned bronze skin, and blue
eyes, with small blue gem earrings she keeps on throughout. Tall and
curvy-athletic — a full bust, toned waist, and wide hips. (One of two tanned
characters alongside nurse [Linh Nguyen](Linh-Nguyen) — but Nubia's hair is short
and white where Linh's is a black bob.)

**Signature look across the levels.** As a parent Nubia wears her own clothes, and
her taste is bold, young, and rock-casual — a consistent red-top-and-denim palette
with teal slip-on trainers and the blue earrings at every level. At level 1 it's a
cropped red bell-sleeve sweater and high-waisted jeans, midriff already bare; by
level 5 a cropped red top with black shorts; at level 7 a red tank and cuffed denim
shorts; by level 10 a cropped red shrug over bare breasts above tiny heart-belted
denim micro-shorts. Crops, denim, and that red-and-teal combo throughout — a
confident, rock-casual look that matches her blunt, strong-willed streak.

## Personality

A very direct, strong-willed woman with a big personality and a bit of a rocker
attitude. Nubia says what she thinks without softening it and doesn't back down
easily, and she's got a short fuse: push her or waste her time and the bluntness
turns confrontational fast. At the PTA that bluntness cuts through the dithering — she's the one who'll
weigh a contested question and then just move it toward a decision rather than let
it circle.

**Level progression.** Her forthright, unshakeable streak stays constant as the
campus climate rises (see [School Levels](School-Levels)); she meets whatever
comes head-on rather than clutching her pearls about it.
